Step 1: Start with a MnCHOICES assessment

Minnesota's public programs start with a MnCHOICES assessment. Contact your county (or tribal nation, or health plan if your loved one is enrolled in one) and ask for an assessment for home and community-based services. It decides which programs your loved one qualifies for.

CADI (Community Access for Disability Inclusion)

A Medical Assistance waiver for people under 65 with a certified disability who need the level of care a nursing home provides. At EverHaven, that usually means residents ages 55 to 64. CADI can pay for the care services in assisted living.

Elderly Waiver (EW)

A Medical Assistance program for people 65 and older who need nursing-home-level care but can be served in the community. Like CADI, it can pay for care services in assisted living. Talk with us early, since every EW case is reviewed individually.

Housing Support (formerly GRH)

Waivers generally pay for care, not rent. Housing Support is a state program that can help adults with low income pay for room and board in eligible settings, and it is often paired with CADI or EW.

Private pay

Families can also pay privately. The monthly rate depends on the care plan, so we share pricing after the assessment. Long-term care insurance and, for veterans or surviving spouses, VA Aid and Attendance benefits may help cover the cost.

What to gather before you call
  • Medical Assistance status, if any
  • County case manager's name and number
  • Current medication list
  • Primary doctor's contact
  • Power of attorney or guardianship papers

General information only, not legal or financial advice. Program rules change, and your county or health plan makes the final eligibility decision.

Straight answers

Who is EverHaven a good fit for?

Adults 55+ who need anything from light help to high-acuity care, including two-person transfers and behavioral or mental health needs. We don't provide memory care.

What funding do you accept?

CADI, Elderly Waiver, Housing Support (GRH) and private pay. Call us to talk through a specific person's funding.

How fast can someone move in?

Assessment within 24 hours. Once paperwork and authorization are set, move-in can happen the same or next day.

What does it cost?

It depends on the care plan and funding. For private pay, we share a clear monthly rate after the assessment.

Can you serve someone under 55?

Our license is for adults 55 and older. Call us and we'll talk through whether a county-approved exception is possible.

Is EverHaven licensed?

Yes. Minnesota Department of Health Assisted Living License #422593.
